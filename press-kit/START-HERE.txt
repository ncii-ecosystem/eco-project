NCII-ECOSYSTEM.ORG PRESS KIT

WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?

NCII-ECOSYSTEM.ORG maps 11 categories of technology that enable the creation, distribution, discovery, support, and monetization of AI-generated non-consensual intimate images. We turned our research into an interactive story and a living evidence database to help people understand the systems behind deepfake abuse and identify where interventions are possible.

Website — https://ncii-ecosystem.org
Research paper — https://doi.org/10.1145/3846167.3848604
