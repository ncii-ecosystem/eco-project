How to use this database

  • Search for a topic, author, or source, and narrow results by technology or type. Sort by date or title, and open an entry to read its original source.
  • Choosing several technologies matches entries related to any of them. Technology, type, and search filters work together.
  • Export your current results as CSV for further analysis.
  • Submit a source we’re missing through “Submit entries” to help grow the collection. You can contribute anonymously or choose to be credited.

Methodology

We group sources using the framework from our research paper: creation, distribution, proliferation and discovery, infrastructural support, and monetization. A source can cover more than one technology.

The database brings together papers, articles, reports, and other sources we’ve collected so far. It doesn’t cover everything, and several entries may discuss the same event. Counts show how many sources we’ve collected, not how often harm occurs or which technologies matter most.

Submission moderation policy

Researchers review each submission before it appears here, so your entry won’t be visible right away. We check that the source is relevant, the details are accurate, and it isn’t already listed. Submissions that meet these criteria are added to the collection.
