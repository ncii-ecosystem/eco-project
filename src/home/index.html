We are mapping the tech ecosystem behind deepfake abuse.

Deepfake abuse is not just a single image, model, website, or app. It's an entire ecosystem. To protect women, girls, LGBTQ+ communities, and other vulnerable groups online, we need to see this ecosystem clearly. In Ding et al. (2026) (ACM EAAMO '26), we contribute the first ecosystem of 11 technologies that facilitate the creation, distribution, proliferation and discovery, infrastructural support, and monetization of AI-generated non-consensual intimate images (AIG-NCII) through a synthesis of 100+ sources across academia, industry, journalism, law & policy, trust & safety, and civil society. This project is the next step. We translate the research paper into a visual storytelling project and a living evidence database to educate and raise awareness, promote science communication, and support future interventions. This project is affiliated with the Center for Technological Responsibility (CNTR) at Brown University.
