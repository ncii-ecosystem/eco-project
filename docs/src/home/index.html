What brings you here?

This website communicates the findings of a research paper on the technologies that facilitate AI-generated non-consensual intimate images. Our goal is to make that research easier to understand and act on by offering practical ways to learn, find support, and explore the evidence.

I need support↗ Find survivor-centered support, removal tools, and guidance. I want to learn↗ Follow an interactive story about how AI-generated intimate-image abuse spreads. I’m researching this issue↗ Search the evidence database. I want to read the paper↗ Read the research and its ecosystem framework. I want to contribute↗ Add a source or resource to the evidence database.
