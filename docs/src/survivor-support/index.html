If you or someone you know has been affected by intimate images made or shared without consent, including images made with AI, these resources offer emotional support, guidance on image removal and legal options, and links to local services.

Personal support & safety

Lila.help

Location: Worldwide

A directory of trusted local helplines and organizations offering support after gender-based violence, including sexual violence, in almost every country.

lila.help ↗ (opens in a new tab)

StopNCII.org — Global Network of Partners

Location: Worldwide

A global network of advocates and nonprofits supporting people affected by intimate image abuse.

stopncii.org ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Cyber Civil Rights Initiative

Location: United States; international resource directory

Support for exploring next steps, saving evidence, and finding legal information or emotional support after intimate image abuse.

U.S. Image Abuse Helpline: 1-844-878-2274.

cybercivilrights.org ↗ (opens in a new tab)

RAINN (Rape, Abuse & Incest National Network)

Location: United States

Confidential emotional support for people affected by sexual violence and those supporting them. Offers step-by-step guidance on saving evidence, reporting intimate images made with AI, requesting removal, and protecting accounts.

U.S. hotline: 1-800-656-HOPE (4673).
Text HOPE to 64673 or use online chat (opens in a new tab).

rainn.org ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Revenge Porn Helpline

Location: United Kingdom

Support for people aged 18 or over affected by intimate image abuse, including images made with AI. Offers guidance on reporting, requesting removal, and exploring next steps.

UK helpline: 0345 6000 459.
Email: help@revengepornhelpline.org.uk.

revengepornhelpline.org.uk ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Netsafe

Location: New Zealand

Confidential support after intimate image abuse, including help with reporting, requesting removal, and exploring next steps.

NZ helpline: 0508 638 723.
Text Netsafe to 4282.
Email: help@netsafe.org.nz.

netsafe.org.nz ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Intimate Images Protection Service

Location: Canada (British Columbia)

Emotional support and guidance on removal and legal options after intimate images are shared, or someone threatens to share them. Includes images made with AI.

takebackyourimages.gov.bc.ca ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Image removal

StopNCII.org

Location: Worldwide

A tool that helps participating websites find and remove intimate images shared without consent, including images made with AI. For images showing someone aged 18 or older; the person pictured uses the tool.

stopncii.org ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Take It Down

Location: Worldwide

A tool for intimate images taken when someone was under 18, even if they are now an adult. Helps participating websites find and remove matching images.

takeitdown.ncmec.org ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Google intimate-image removal

Location: Worldwide; Google services

Request removal of intimate images shared without consent, including images made with AI, from Google Search and supported Google services. Removing a search result does not remove the image from the original website.

support.google.com ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Microsoft / Bing image removal

Location: Worldwide; Microsoft services

Request removal of intimate images shared without consent, including images made with AI, from Bing search results and Microsoft services.

microsoft.com ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Reporting abuse

NCMEC CyberTipline

Location: Worldwide; based in the United States

A place to report suspected sexual abuse or exploitation of children, including sexual images made with AI. Reports may be shared with law enforcement.

U.S. phone: 1-800-843-5678.

report.cybertip.org ↗ (opens in a new tab)

FTC — Take It Down reporting

Location: United States

A place to report websites or apps that have not removed intimate images after a removal request. Explains when the 48-hour removal requirement applies, including to images made with AI.

takeitdown.ftc.gov ↗ (opens in a new tab)

eSafety Commissioner

Location: Australia

A service for reporting intimate images shared without consent, or threats to share them. Can help seek removal, including for images made with AI.

Start an online report.

esafety.gov.au ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Legal information

Rights of Women

Location: England and Wales

Information about intimate image abuse laws, legal rights, and reporting options, including for images made with AI.

Advice line for women: 020 7251 8887.

rightsofwomen.org.uk ↗ (opens in a new tab)

WomensLaw

Location: United States

Plain-language information about intimate image abuse and legal options. An email service answers questions about U.S. law.

Email Hotline.

womenslaw.org ↗ (opens in a new tab)
